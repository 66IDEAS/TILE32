# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

TILE32 contributors are expected to provide a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free environment. Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, and
focus discussion on the work. Maintainers may remove abusive content or exclude
participants whose conduct undermines the community.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project
maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate
confidentiality.
